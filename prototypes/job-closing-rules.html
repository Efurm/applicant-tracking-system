What happens when a job opening closes?

This demo explores Grill Question 8. When an Admin closes a job opening that still has applicants in progress (Applied → Offer), what should happen to those applicants? And can a closed job be reopened? Pick a rule below, then click through the walkthroughs or use free play. Watch for moments where you think "wait, that shouldn't be allowed" or "that's annoying."
